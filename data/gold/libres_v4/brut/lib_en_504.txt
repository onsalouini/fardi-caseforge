Kantaoui Foundry Co. is located in the heart of Manouba, specializing in the production of hardened pinions for various industries. We recently noticed an increase in energy consumption during the welding process of our robots. The current energy usage stands at 480 kWh per tonne, which is higher than our competitors’ rate of 340 kWh per tonne.

The welding robot has been in operation for two years now and is critical for our productivity. We need your help to investigate this issue and come up with solutions to reduce the energy usage without compromising quality or speed. A changeover must not exceed 15 minutes, and we can only conduct trials on Saturdays to minimize disruption.

We would like the students to gather data on the energy consumption patterns, identify potential areas for improvement, and propose modifications or new methods that could reduce our energy use. They should also explore ways to ensure that any changes do not affect the overall production time.

We request that the students adhere to strict safety guidelines and that all experiments comply with the total productive maintenance standards. Trials can only be conducted on Saturdays, as mentioned earlier.
