Kantaoui Packaging Works is a leading manufacturer of woven bags in Mateur. We have recently installed a new pellet dryer to enhance our production process. However, we are facing an issue with our workforce attendance which has been affecting our operations.

The absenteeism rate among our employees stands at 9%, which is higher than the industry standard of 6%. This has led to delays in production and a decrease in overall efficiency. To address this issue, we require a comprehensive study on the current situation and potential solutions.

We have access to various datasets that can provide insights into the problem. These include employee attendance records, production timelines, and maintenance logs. The data can be accessed from our company’s intranet under the 'HR' section, labeled as 26M745.

We would like the final-year students to conduct a detailed analysis of these data to identify the root causes of the high absenteeism rate. Additionally, they should propose and evaluate different strategies to reduce absenteeism and improve productivity. The study must last no more than six weeks to ensure timely delivery.

Any changes or adjustments proposed must be approved by the end customer before implementation. This will ensure that the solutions are practical and align with our operational needs.
