Sbeitla Laboratories Co. is a leading pharmaceutical company based in El Jem. We specialize in the production and distribution of various medicinal products, with a focus on suppositories.

A non-conformity rate of 11% has been identified in our production line for suppositories. This is a concerning issue that needs to be addressed immediately. Our current high-shear granulator has shown inefficiencies in producing consistent quality products.

We have the following data available: production records, quality control reports, and maintenance logs. These will be provided to the students for their analysis.

The task assigned to the students is to investigate the root cause of the non-conformity and propose solutions to reduce it. They should use the 5 Whys method to identify the underlying issues.

The investment in new equipment or improvements is capped at TND 40,000. Therefore, any proposed solutions must be within this budget constraint. Additionally, we need to ensure that the BRCGS certification remains intact during any changes made to the production process.
